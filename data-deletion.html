Data Deletion Request

MattAi Salon · Last updated: 12 July 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal data associated with your conversations with a participating salon through MattAi Salon. This includes your message history and any booking details linked to your messaging profile.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to admin@hadacorporation.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Tell us which salon you contacted and the messaging platform you used (Instagram, Facebook Messenger, or LINE), and the display name or handle you messaged from, so we can locate your data.
  3. We will verify the request and delete the associated conversation and booking data.

What gets deleted

  • Your message history stored by the Service.
  • Booking records linked to your messaging profile (upcoming appointments will be cancelled as part of this).
  • The platform-provided identifier used to route your replies.

Timing

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some records may be retained for a limited period only where required by law, after which they are removed.

Questions

If you have any question about this process, contact us at admin@hadacorporation.com. For more on how we handle data, see our Privacy Policy.
