Privacy Policy

MattAi Salon · Last updated: 12 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MattAi Salon (the “Service”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when a customer messages a participating hair salon through Instagram, Facebook Messenger, or LINE. The Service is an AI-powered booking assistant that helps salons answer enquiries and manage appointments automatically.

In short: We only process the messages a customer sends to a salon and the booking details needed to schedule an appointment. We do not sell personal data, and customers can request deletion at any time.

1. Who we are

The Service is operated as a messaging automation tool on behalf of participating hair salons. Each salon is the business the customer chooses to contact; the Service processes messages solely to provide that salon’s booking and enquiry responses.

2. Information we collect

When a customer sends a message to a participating salon, we may process:

  • Message content — the text a customer sends (for example, an enquiry about prices, or a request to book, change, or cancel an appointment).
  • Platform-provided identifiers — the messaging platform’s user ID (for example, an Instagram-scoped ID) and public display name, used to route replies to the correct conversation.
  • Booking details — information the customer provides to make an appointment, such as preferred date, time, service, and stylist.

We do not collect passwords, payment card numbers, or contacts. We do not access a customer’s follower list, media, or other profile data beyond what is described above.

3. How we use information

  • To read and understand a customer’s message and generate a helpful reply.
  • To create, look up, change, or cancel appointments at the customer’s request.
  • To notify salon staff when a customer needs human assistance.
  • To answer frequently asked questions (location, hours, pricing, and similar).

Message text may be processed by a third-party AI language model provider solely to generate the reply. It is not used to train models and is not shared for advertising.

4. Legal basis and consent

We process a customer’s message because the customer has chosen to contact the salon and expects a response. Processing is limited to what is necessary to answer the enquiry and manage the requested booking.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with:

  • The salon the customer contacted, so staff can serve the customer.
  • Service providers that operate the infrastructure (messaging platform APIs, the workflow automation host, the database host, and the AI language model provider), strictly to deliver the Service.

6. Data retention

Booking records are kept for as long as needed to manage appointments and the salon’s ongoing relationship with the customer. Conversation logs are retained only as long as necessary to operate and troubleshoot the Service, then removed. A customer may request earlier deletion at any time (see below).

7. Data deletion & your rights

Customers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. To request deletion, follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page, or contact us using the details below. We will remove the associated conversation and booking data upon a verified request.

8. Data security

Access tokens and personal data are stored in access-controlled systems. Access is limited to what is required to operate the Service. Communication with messaging platforms uses secure, encrypted connections.

9. Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for salon customers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision.
